Pitchroom AI is a source-grounded, voice-first pitch rehearsal assistant.

Pitchroom AI solves the problem that static pitch decks can leave founders unable to answer unexpected questions in live meetings.

When an investor or customer asks an unexpected question, founders can lose time searching slides or improvise a claim that was never approved.

Pitchroom AI helps founder-led startup teams prepare for investor meetings, customer conversations, and internal demos.

Pitchroom AI turns an approved pitch deck or document into a voice conversation by accepting PPTX, selectable-text PDF, DOCX, Markdown, or plain-text sources.

For PPTX files, Pitchroom keeps slide numbers and speaker notes attached to the extracted source sections. For PDFs, it keeps page numbers attached. Image-only slides, scanned pages, and chart screenshots are surfaced as review warnings instead of being treated as reliable evidence.

A live voice turn starts after one microphone permission, ends automatically after a brief pause, is transcribed, matched to relevant source sections, answered from those sections, and returned as spoken audio.

During a spoken reply, a presenter can speak over Pitchroom to interrupt playback and ask the next question; the microphone keeps listening through that new turn.

The current Pitchroom AI experience provides seamless turn-taking, not word-by-word streaming transcription.

Pitchroom AI stays trustworthy by declining when it finds no matching approved-source evidence, and it does not invent traction, pricing, fundraising, or customer facts that are absent from the pitch.

The hackathon prototype supports selectable text from PPTX, PDF, and DOCX sources plus Markdown and plain-text files. It does not yet understand image-only slides, charts, or scanned documents without a review warning.

The active pitch is held in memory for the demo; a production version would add authenticated workspaces, versioning, and encrypted storage.

The working prototype includes document extraction, source-section retrieval, typed questions, browser silence detection, interruption-aware voice turns, provider-backed transcription, and provider-backed speech output.

Pitchroom AI's promise is simple: every answer, from the deck.
