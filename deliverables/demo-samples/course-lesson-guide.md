# Fieldnotes Studio: Better customer interviews
Fictional course notes for learners who want to run clearer customer interviews and turn what they hear into useful findings.

## Course outline
The course has four lessons: plan an interview, ask open questions, synthesize evidence, and share findings. Lesson 3 is called “From notes to findings.”

## Lesson 3: From notes to findings
Use three steps: highlight the participant’s exact words, group observations that describe the same behavior, then write one finding supported by those observations. Keep the finding focused on what the participant did and why it mattered.

A finding describes observed behavior in context and the consequence for the participant. A feature idea proposes a solution. Record the finding first; keep solution ideas in a separate list.

## Practice activity
The practice takes 12 minutes. Choose one anonymized interview quote, add the situation around it, and write a one-sentence finding. The exercise is complete when the sentence describes behavior and cites the quote that supports it.

## Trainer notes
Ask learners, “What was the person trying to get done?” before discussing a product change. Do not treat one interview as proof that every customer has the same need.
