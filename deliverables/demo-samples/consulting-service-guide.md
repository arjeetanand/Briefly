# Northstar Research Lab: Service guide
Fictional example for a small research consultancy. All service details, dates, and prices below are illustrative, not a real offer.

## The Research Sprint
The four-week Research Sprint helps a product team understand how customers complete one agreed task. Work starts after the client names a decision owner and approves the interview plan.

## Included deliverables
The sprint includes a 90-minute kickoff, six moderated 45-minute customer interviews, a concise findings report, and a 60-minute readout. The client receives anonymized notes and the evidence-backed findings report; raw recordings are not included.

## Scope and exclusions
The fixed scope covers one product area and one customer segment. Participant recruiting, translation, prototype design, and additional interview rounds are outside scope. The team confirms any added work and its cost in writing before it begins.

## Timeline and communication
Delivery takes four weeks after kickoff and approval of the interview plan. Research lead Mina sends a written progress note each Friday. The client supplies the decision owner and participant contacts before scheduling.

## Illustrative commercial terms
The fictional sample fee is $8,500. Half is due at kickoff and half after the readout. Travel is excluded from the example fee.
